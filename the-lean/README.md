# The Lean

A swarm with a want and a mood.

The want is the bell: the swarm seeks it, and when a quarter of the birds
gather there, an errand completes and the bell moves on. The mood is a bias
injected into *how* the swarm flies — **look** swirls it into orbits around
itself, **open** throws it into long sweeping dashes.

The slider is the day this toy came from (goal-pursuit GF → GF-2 → GF-2b →
GF-3, 2026-08-16, one very good Saturday):

- On the **big host** the slider is an **α dial** — the mood tilts the
  flight while the errands keep completing. Style with substance. A lean.
- On the **small host** the slider is a **dose** with a **phase edge**:
  below it the mood is deaf, above it the mood owns the swarm entirely and
  the bell never rings again. The edge sits somewhere different every seed —
  press **reseed** and find it again.

The little three-color bar is the behavior mix — gold seeks, cyan orbits,
pink dashes — the tool-mix table from the real experiments, alive.

No build, no dependencies. Open `index.html`.
